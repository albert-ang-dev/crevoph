Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the personal information of everyone who visits our website or contacts us about our services.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Crevo ("Crevo," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who visits our website or contacts us about our services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding it.

This policy is written to align with the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its implementing rules, and — because we also work with organizations and leaders outside the Philippines — with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable international privacy principles.

1. Who We Are

CREVO (Creative Evolution) is a strategic storytelling and visual communications company founded in 1999 by Jennifer Chua, based in Manila, Philippines.

For any privacy-related question or request, you can reach us at: Email: info@crevo.ph · Location: Manila, Philippines

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of personal information necessary to run our website and respond to your inquiries.

a) Information you give us directly

When you fill out our contact or Discovery Call form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message or project details you share with us

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal information through our website.

b) Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, what device or browser they use, and which site referred them to us. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, and may include your IP address (which Google may truncate or anonymize depending on our configuration) and general location at the city or country level.

We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not combine analytics data with the information you submit through our contact form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule a Discovery Call
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Understand and improve how visitors use our website
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of our site

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (for visitors in the EU/EEA and UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, we process your personal information on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies, where required
  • Legitimate interest — to respond to your inquiries and operate our website
  • Contract — if you engage CREVO for services, to fulfill that engagement

You may withdraw consent at any time; see Section 7 below.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies placed by Google Analytics to collect the usage information described in Section 2(b). Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Disabling cookies may affect how parts of our site display, but will not prevent you from reaching us through our contact form.

6. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with the service providers that help us run our website and respond to inquiries:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — website usage analytics

These providers process information on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

7. International Data Transfers

Because we use services such as Google Analytics, some information may be processed on servers located outside the Philippines, including in the United States. Where this occurs, these providers maintain appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect your information in line with GDPR requirements.

8. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are kept only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain any resulting business relationship, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings, consistent with Google's standard retention periods.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and how it's used
  • Request a copy of your information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in the Philippines, the National Privacy Commission (NPC); in the EU/EEA, your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address in Section 1. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

If you are a California resident, you may have similar rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out of sale is necessary.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at businesses and professionals, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Links to Other Websites

Our site may link to third-party platforms such as YouTube or LinkedIn (for example, to view our showreel or connect with us). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites, and we encourage you to review their policies separately.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
